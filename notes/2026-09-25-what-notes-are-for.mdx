A note is meant to be cheaper than a blog post: something you worked out, a
gotcha worth remembering, a paragraph that would otherwise be lost in a
scratch file. No obligation to be complete, polished, or finished.

{/* truncate */}

## Writing one

Drop a file in `notes/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-some-slug.mdx` with a `title` in the
front matter. That is the whole workflow — same as `blog/`, different folder.

Add `draft: true` to the front matter to keep a note out of production builds
while still seeing it in `npm run start`.
